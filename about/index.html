About Mosaic Twist

An original tile-twisting puzzle game for iPhone.

How It Plays

Every tile belongs somewhere—but only in the right place and rotation. Move tiles between the board and tile bank, swap pieces, and turn them in precise 90-degree steps until every neighboring edge connects.

Start with approachable Easy boards, move into Standard puzzles, and take on Advanced challenges when you’re ready.

Hundreds of Puzzles Across Multiple Worlds

Explore dozens of campaigns set across multiple distinctive worlds. Each brings its own visual style and gameplay soundtrack, while progress-based unlocks give every solved board somewhere to lead.

One Shared Daily Twist

A new Daily Twist is assigned to each calendar date, giving everyone the same solver-validated challenge for that day. Today’s puzzle is free to play, and Mosaic Twist keeps an offline fallback ready if the date service cannot be reached.

Your Daily results, streak, and history stay on your device. After a solve, copy and share a spoiler-free text result with its color grid, time, twists, Hints, and current streak when applicable. Optional Daily reminders are local notifications and are off until you choose to enable them.

Create and Revisit with Mosaic Twist Pro

Mosaic Twist Pro is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription. It opens Past Puzzles so you can play missed dates, revisit saved results, and replay eligible completed dates.

Pro also unlocks Custom Puzzles with your choice of difficulty, grid size, bundled tile set, match variety, and locked tiles. Unlimited Hints apply in Campaign, Custom, and Daily Twist; unlimited Auto-Solves apply in Campaign and Custom. Daily Auto-Solve remains unavailable.

Pro can open campaigns early through eligible progression locks. All currently bundled campaigns and artwork sets remain Free, and Pro does not promise access to future separately offered content.

Play Your Way

Move tiles with taps or drags, then rotate with a tap or two-finger twist. Choose the tools you want—including Check, Hint, Reset, Solve, and Shuffle—and arrange them to fit the way you play. Progress and best times stay on your device.

Mosaic Twist is free to play, with no ads and no account. Campaigns, bundled puzzles, and saved progress are designed for offline play.
