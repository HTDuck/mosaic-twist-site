Gameplay

Simple moves, satisfying matches.

Every tile belongs somewhere—but only in the right place and rotation. Move, swap, and twist tiles until every neighboring edge connects.

1

Move

Tap or drag tiles between the board and tile bank, then swap pieces as you refine.

2

Twist

Rotate tiles with a tap or two-finger twist, one precise quarter-turn at a time.

3

Solve

Match every neighboring edge and watch the mosaic come together.

More ways to play

A new twist every day—or one of your own.

Keep the classic Campaign journey, meet the shared puzzle of the day, or shape a challenge around the way you want to play.

Daily Twist

One puzzle. Every day.

Take on the same Daily Twist for each calendar date. Track your time, twists, Hints, and streak, then copy and share a spoiler-free result.

Today’s Daily Twist is free, and its puzzle remains playable with the app’s offline fallback.
